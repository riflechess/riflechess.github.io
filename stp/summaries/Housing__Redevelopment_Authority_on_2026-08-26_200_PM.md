#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Housing & Redevelopment Authority (HRA) Meeting
**Purpose:** Review of the 2027 proposed budget, allocation of specific housing funds, and Tax Increment Financing (TIF) updates.
**Date/Time:** August 26, 2026, at 2:00 PM.

#### Summary
The meeting focused heavily on the 2027 budget proposals for the Housing & Redevelopment Authority, specifically regarding how tax revenues are redistributed to support city growth and housing stability.

**Key Budget & Development Decisions:**
*   **Sales Tax Revitalization (STAR):** The budget for 2027 will move away from the "commercial corridor" program, as that was a one-time expenditure. Additionally, funds are being prioritized for "Neighborhood" and "Cultural" STAR programs.
*   **Arena Investment:** A significant investment (over $5 million total) is planned for the arena complex. 
*   **Affordable Housing Assistance:** Significant funds are earmarked for local affordable housing, including $1 million for down payment assistance, $1 million for an inheritance fund, and $1 million for emergency rental assistance.
*   **Housing Expansion:** The HRA is moving forward with ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) subsidies to increase housing density and is funding a "St. Paul Immigration Context Survey" to identify and preserve historic resources.
*   **Community Programs:** The "Healthy Homes" program (improving home conditions for 1-4 unit homes) will continue through a mix of Trust Fund and ARPA dollars.
*   **TIF Update:** The city plans to decertify several TIF districts, which is expected to release approximately $4.6 million back to the local tax base. These funds, along with others, are being "pooled" to support approximately 1,000 new units of rental and ownership housing.

**Public Safety & Quality of Life:**
*   **Rental Assistance Hotline:** To ensure equitable service, the city is moving toward an RFP (Request for Proposal) process for the rental assistance hotline rather than a direct contract.
*   **Property Maintenance:** The Parking Enterprise Fund will continue to be used for the capital repairs and maintenance of downtown parking ramps to ensure they remain operational and safe.

**Moments of Contention:**
*   **Arena Funding Transparency:** There was significant pushback regarding the lack of detailed documentation for the $5 million+ arena investment. Commissioner Noecker expressed frustration that the information was not readily available in the budget write-up. 
*   **Delayed Information:** A point of contention arose when staff suggested waiting until a team member returned from leave (mid-September) to provide specific details on the arena funding. Commissioner Noecker argued that because a budget vote is scheduled for late September, the city cannot wait a month for information that was proposed just last month. She requested a written response much sooner to ensure the board can make an informed decision.
