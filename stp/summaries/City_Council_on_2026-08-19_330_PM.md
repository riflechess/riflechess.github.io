#### Meeting Info
**Type:** City Council Meeting  
**Purpose:** Discussion of city ordinances, budgetary resolutions, public safety measures, and property management.  
**Date/Time:** August 19, 2026, at 3:30 PM.

#### Summary
The meeting covered a range of topics including municipal leadership transitions, infrastructure improvements, and several housing-related enforcement actions.

*   **Fire Chief Selection (Ordinance 26-38):** The Council reviewed the formation of a selection committee to find a new Fire Chief. A representative from the local firefighters' union spoke in favor of the current candidate slate, praising its diversity and depth of experience. The council moved to lay this item over until next week for final consideration.
*   **Public Safety & Technology Concerns:** There was significant public outcry regarding the use of drones (specifically Skydio brand) and surveillance technology. Residents expressed concerns about lack of transparency in contracts, the "militarization" of police equipment, and the impact on community privacy. While several budget adjustments were approved by the council to account for various capital projects, the testimony highlighted a distinct community desire for more oversight regarding how public funds are spent on high-tech policing tools.
*   **Infrastructure & Development:** 
    *   **Sewer Connection Charges:** A special sanitary sewer availability connection charge district was established for "Como Use." This ensures that developers and new property owners in the area pay their share of improvements to aging infrastructure, rather than existing residents.
    *   **Road Work:** The council reviewed a mill and overlay program for Front Avenue; this item was continued until October 7th for further review.
*   **Community Property & Blight Control:**
    *   **Emergent Boarding:** Two properties were assessed for costs associated with emergency boarding following break-ins and vandalism. These cases highlighted the need for rapid response to secure buildings during off-hours.
    *   **Property Improvements (947 Fremont Ave):** This property has been in a cycle of stalled renovations. The council is using a "monthly fee" system to encourage the owner to complete work, though they granted a two-week delay on certain reports to allow for better inspection timelines.
    *   **Vehicle Abatement (314 Wyoming St):** A local owner requested one extra week to remove vehicles from a property due to issues obtaining titles; the council granted this extension.
*   **Utility & Safety Issues:** For 1103 Chatsworth Street, a resident spoke up about being cut off from utilities due to an old debt not in her name. The Council extended a deadline for resolution to October 15th to allow more time for her to access resources and negotiate with the utility company.
*   **Community Events:** Sound variances were approved for local events: the "Walk for Recovery" (Sept. 12) and the "Nativity County Fair" (Sept. 18-20).
