#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Housing & Redevelopment Authority Meeting
**Purpose:** Review of housing projects, funding transfers, and property sales.
**Date/Time:** October 14, 2026, at 2:00 PM
**Context:** The meeting included discussions on the Afton View Apartments, the Right Track youth workforce program, and the Maryland Park property sale.

#### Summary
**Afton View Apartments Project**
The board voted unanimously (6-0) to approve a partial waiver of the Sustainable Building Ordinance and the issuance of revenue bonds for the Afton View Apartments (371 and 373 Winthrop Street South, etc.). 
*   **Note on Developer Fees:** There was a detailed discussion regarding the policy on developer's fees. Commissioners suggested that in the future, staff should provide clear charts showing how different fee levels impact construction costs to ensure that fee adjustments don't inadvertently increase financing costs or create "inverted" costs for projects.

**Right Track Youth Workforce Program (Funding Discussion)**
A report was presented regarding the $191,000 annual transfer from the HRA to the Parks and Recreation department to fund the "Right Track" program.
*   **Program Impact:** The program is highly successful, having paid out approximately $2 million in wages to local youth this year and managing over 760 internship placements.
*   **Point of Contention/Concern:** There was significant discussion regarding the **sustainability of this funding**. Because the program has been tied to "special funds" for over a decade, commissioners expressed concern that the program is becoming overly reliant on a source that is naturally depleting. 
*   **Future Strategy:** There was a call for a more robust long-term funding strategy, specifically looking into how the city can better engage the private sector and local business community to provide investment, similar to models seen in other cities.

**Maryland Park Property Sale**
A proposal was presented for the sale of the Maryland Park Apartments (1619 Maryland Avenue East).
*   **Transaction Details:** The property, consisting of 172 units, is being sold to Ledge Capital for $19.3 million. 
*   **Loan Assumption:** The buyer is requesting to assume an existing $600,000 HRA loan (extending the maturity to 2056) and a new first mortgage of $17.5 million.
*   **Affordability:** All units (100%) are confirmed at 60% of the Area Median Income (AMI).
*   **Next Steps:** This item will move to a final board vote on October 28th.
