#### Meeting Info
*   **Meeting Type:** City Council Meeting
*   **Purpose:** Regular legislative session and public hearings
*   **Date/Time:** September 2, 2026, at 3:30 PM
*   **Location:** Saint Paul City Hall

#### Summary
The meeting covered several key items regarding public safety, city infrastructure, and community recognition.

**Significant Points of Contention & Delays**
*   **Police Drone Program Grant (Resolution 26-1399):** The Council discussed a proposal to apply for a \$200,000 federal grant to expand the existing police drone program. This item was highly debated due to concerns regarding surveillance, public trust, and the speed at which the proposal was introduced. Several council members expressed that they were not sufficiently prepared to make a final decision because of the lack of information regarding the long-term impact on community relations. **The council voted for a one-week layover** to allow members more time to review the specifics of the contract and the department's data-sharing policies.
*   **Property Dispute at 777 Ohio Street (Resolution 26-158):** This item involved a \$6,800 assessment for sewer work. During the public hearing, residents provided testimony disputing the charges, claiming that the city's work was not performed as described and that the bill was inflated. Because of these significant factual discrepancies between the city's records and the owner's evidence, the council **voted to refer the matter back to legislative hearings** for a deeper investigation before a final decision is made.

**Public Safety and Infrastructure**
*   **Fire Department Budget Adjustments:** The Council approved several adjustments to the fire department’s spending plans, including:
    *   A \$108,118.15 adjustment for firefighter training costs.
    *   A \$8,500 adjustment following the sale of a department trailer.
    *   A \$37,000 adjustment following the sale of land at 1683 Energy Park Drive.
*   **Performance Deposit (947 Fremont Avenue):** The council approved a modified plan to forfeit portions of a performance deposit for a contractor who has failed to complete repairs on a site.

**Community Highlights and Appointments**
*   **Staff Appointments:** The city officially welcomed and swore in three new staff members: Corinne Dennis (Communications Lead), Reggie Evans (Reparations Legislative Aide), and Joanna Zimney (Legislative Hearing Officer).
*   **JC Johnson Becker Day:** The council officially proclaimed September 2, 2026, as "JC Johnson Becker Day" to honor a local youth's significant contributions to the State of the Youth Address.
*   **State of the Youth Address:** A report was provided on the inaugural event, which featured poetry and speeches from local youth regarding housing, climate, and community safety.
*   **Community Announcement:** A backpack giveaway for local students is scheduled for September 10, from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM at the Hmong American Partnership building.

**Public Hearings (Unanimously Approved)**
*   Sound level variances were granted for several upcoming events, including **TommyFest** (Sept 25), **Creighton-Durham Hall football games** (Oct 8 & Oct 21), and a **Walk for Water** event (Sept 26).
