#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Policy Committee Meeting
**Purpose:** To discuss a proposed dust mitigation ordinance for the Southport Industrial area and to review the legislative structure/independence of the City Council’s legal counsel.
**Date/Time:** September 16, 2026, at 1:00 PM
**Context:** City of Saint Paul meeting involving community advocates, local residents, and legal experts.

#### Summary

**Southport Dust Mitigation Ordinance**
The committee heard a presentation regarding the significant impact of fugitive dust in the Southport area. Residents, particularly in the 55107 zip code, reported severe quality-of-life issues, including:
*   **Public Health:** High rates of asthma-related emergency room visits, especially among children.
*   **Property Damage:** Persistent dust buildup on homes and vehicles.
*   **Environmental Justice:** The area is identified as an environmental justice zone where lower-income residents and people of color bear a disproportionate burden of industrial pollution.

The proposed ordinance would require facilities in the industrial area with unpaved roads to create and implement a "fugitive dust plan." These plans would include specific control procedures, staff training, and requirements for public fact sheets. The committee discussed the need for a local ordinance to complement state (MPCA) rules, providing the city with more direct authority to enforce compliance. The Port Authority confirmed they currently contract for weekly sweeping but acknowledged that a specific ordinance could provide stronger enforcement mechanisms.

**City Council Legal Counsel Analysis**
The committee reviewed a study regarding the role of the City Attorney. The primary discussion points included:
*   **Current Structure:** Under the current city charter, the City Attorney represents the "city" as an entity. This means the attorney serves both the city's operations and the council's legislative actions.
*   **The "Independence" Issue:** There was a discussion regarding whether the City Council needs a separate, independent legal counsel to represent the council's specific interests during policy debates. 
*   **Proposed Solutions:**
    *   **Option A (No Charter Change):** Increasing the budget and headcount for the existing legal team to improve "capacity" and ensure the council isn't delayed by lack of staff.
    *   **Option B (Charter Change):** Creating a legally separate office for the council, though this would require a complex and potentially lengthy update to the city’s charter.

**Items of Note & Contention**
*   **Enforcement Concerns:** There was significant discussion regarding how to make the dust ordinance "real" for residents. The goal is to ensure the city has the tools to act quickly on complaints rather than waiting for state-level intervention.
*   **Policy Development:** The committee agreed to move forward with drafting language for the dust ordinance, acknowledging the urgency of the public health concerns in Southport.
*   **Legal Clarity:** While the council is interested in "independence" for legal counsel, it was noted that any move toward a separate counsel would require a significant overhaul of the city's legal structure.
