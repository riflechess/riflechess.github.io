#### Meeting Info
**Type:** City Council Meeting
**Purpose:** Legislative updates, zoning changes, fee establishments, and public hearings.
**Date/Time:** November 4, 2026, at 3:30 PM.

#### Summary
**Commission Stipends and Transparency**
The Council discussed an ordinance to increase stipends for city commissions and boards. There was a specific point of contention regarding the wording of the ordinance. One council member expressed concern about removing the specific stipend amounts from the public ordinance and replacing them with a "catch-all" clause that allows the City Director and Mayor to set the rates. The concern is that removing the specific figures reduces transparency. Councilmember Coleman expressed openness to feedback on the best mechanism to ensure the stipends are raised while maintaining clear processes.

**Zoning and Solar Access**
Ordinance 26-58 includes several minor zoning updates, with a notable change regarding solar panels. The update aims to make solar panels more accessible for homeowners on narrow lots. Currently, some are forced to stop panels short of the roof edge due to old setback rules; the new language would allow them to use the full roof edge if it makes a difference for the feasibility of the project.

**Economic Development & Business Growth**
The Council approved two items related to a project by Unreal LLC near 400 Snelling Avenue North (the United Village development), including a job creation fund and a Minnesota Investment Fund application. Additionally, the council reviewed a request to rezone property at 1201 Rice Street (Mr. Tire) to a General Business District. This change is intended to support local business, create jobs, and provide easier access to vehicle sales and repairs.

**Public Space & Quality of Life**
*   **Mobile Saunas:** A public hearing was held regarding a new license for mobile saunas on park property. Several community members testified in favor, highlighting the health benefits and the role saunas play in building community.
*   **Street Sweeping:** The council approved a cooperation agreement to enhance street-sweeping activities within the Capital Region Watershed District.
*   **Solar Access:** (As mentioned above) Improvements to zoning code to facilitate solar panel installation.

**2027 Utility and Waste Fees**
The Council reviewed several items regarding the setting of fees for 2027:
*   **Garbage & Recycling:** Provisions were made to update solid waste and recycling fees. A new proposal includes including larger residential properties (5+ units) in the standard fee structure to cover services like waste reduction and "Big Belly" bin maintenance.
*   **Water and Sewer:** The Council moved to set the 2027 rates for water, sanitary sewer, and storm sewer systems.
*   **Energy Initiatives:** The Council approved $150,000 in funding for the 2027 Clean Energy Tax Credit project.
