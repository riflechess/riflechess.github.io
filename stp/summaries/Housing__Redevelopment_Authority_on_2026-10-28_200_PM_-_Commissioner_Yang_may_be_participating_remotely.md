#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Housing & Redevelopment Authority (HRA) Meeting
**Purpose:** Review of property easements, commercial loan modifications, and budget updates.
**Date/Time:** October 28, 2026, at 2:00 PM
**Context:** The meeting involved discussions regarding infrastructure, housing investments, and partnerships with community organizations like Model Cities.

#### Summary
The meeting covered several key items regarding property management and local development:

*   **Smith Avenue Ramp Access:** A request for a temporary easement was approved (4-0 vote). The City of St. Paul needs access for the Capital City bikeway construction, and District Heat and Energy requires access to install new heating and chilled water piping. The project is expected to take about two months and is intended to have minimal impact on HRA parking operations.
*   **Maryland Park Apartments:** A resolution (RES26-1794) was approved (4-0 vote) regarding the assignment and assumption of existing home loans, the extension of maturity dates, and the subordination of a new first mortgage for the property at 1619 Maryland Avenue East.
*   **Model Cities Brownstone Commercial Loans:** This item (SR26-236) involved a lengthy discussion regarding the financial health of a mixed-use building on University Avenue. 
    *   **The Issue:** The current debt burden on the property is too high for the revenue it generates, and many of the minority-owned businesses on the first floor are struggling. Additionally, property taxes on the site doubled recently, causing significant strain.
    *   **Proposed Changes:** To keep the project viable, the HRA and the City are looking to modify the terms of the HRA’s "pass-through" loan from a foundation, as well as the "forgiveness" of two city-held loans (a STAR loan and a CDBG loan) totaling over $650,000.
    *   **Points of Contention/Concern:** There was significant discussion regarding a **$500,000 "recourse obligation."** This means that if the business fails to pay, the HRA would be responsible for that amount. Commissioners expressed concern about the HRA acting as a "middleman" for this debt. However, the current strategy is to restructure the terms so that this risk is not "called" until 2036, allowing the business to stabilize. There was also a firm pushback against "balloon payments," which some members noted are often predatory. 
    *   **Status:** This item was introduced for discussion and will move to a final vote next week.
*   **Quarterly Budget Update:** The presentation regarding the third-quarter budget to actuals (SR26-237) was postponed due to time constraints. This will be heard on the next meeting, November 4th.
