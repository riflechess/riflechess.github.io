#### Meeting Info
- **Type:** Special City Council Meeting
- **Purpose:** To canvass the returns of the 2026 City General Election
- **Date/Time:** November 10, 2026, at 2:00 PM

#### Summary
The meeting featured a strong push for a "Right to Repair" initiative to be implemented in every precinct. The speaker emphasized that this move is a direct response to a "clear mandate" from St. Paul residents. The primary focus is on quality of life and public safety, with the goal of ensuring that all residents have access to safe and dignified living conditions.
