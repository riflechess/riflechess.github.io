#### Meeting Info
- **Type:** City Council Meeting
- **Purpose:** Public meeting to discuss city resolutions, ordinances, public safety, and local infrastructure.
- **Date/Time:** August 26, 2026, at 3:30 PM
- **Context:** Regular meeting of the St. Paul City Council including public hearings on parking, surveillance, and public safety.

#### Summary
**Key Decisions & Infrastructure**
- **Neighborhood House Lease:** The Council approved a breakthrough lease agreement with the state for Neighborhood House, ensuring they can continue providing services to the community.
- **Fire Chief Selection:** An ordinance was adopted to establish a committee to evaluate and recommend candidates for the position of Fire Chief. 
- **Park & Recreation Funding:** Several items were passed regarding parks, including:
    - Acceptance of approximately $70,469.50 for the city's tree planting program through the Green Cities Accord.
    - A $3,922 grant for the Dr. Justice O'Hage Memorial Restoration Project.
    - Acceptance of over $2 million in Legacy Funds for the Parks and Recreation Department.
- **Grants:** The police department was authorized to accept a State Homeland Security Program grant to bolster the 2026 budget.
- **Utility Rates:** The council approved a request from Energy Park Utility Company to adjust their rates.

**Public Safety & Quality of Life**
- **Flock Safety Contract:** The Council passed a resolution to end contracts with Flock Safety. The decision was driven by concerns regarding public trust and the potential for data sharing with third-party organizations that do not meet the city's standards. Members emphasized that while this is a significant step, the city will continue to look for ways to manage surveillance technology and public trust.
- **Sound Variances:** The Council approved three separate applications for sound level variances for upcoming events at the University of St. Thomas, Waldman Brewery, and Church of Lumen Christy.
- **Nuisance Abatement:** A nuisance abatement for a property on Wyoming Street East was officially declared settled and abated.

**Moments of Contention & Specific Discussion**
- **Area 23 Permit Parking (Conflict/Denied):** There was significant discussion regarding a request to expand the permit parking zone in Area 23 (near the University of St. Thomas). While a local representative testified in favor of the expansion to manage the impact of the arena and student parking, **Councilmember Coleman moved to deny the proposal.** The council argued that expanding permit parking is not a solution for underlying issues like lack of enforcement or the need for the university to shift how students travel. The motion to deny the expansion was passed unanimously.
- **Vacant Building Appeal (Discussion):** A homeowner appealed a vacant building registration fee for a property he is attempting to renovate. The council **denied the immediate appeal** regarding the fee waiver, but the council's office will work with the owner to allow him to negotiate a payment plan or a prorated amount during the assessment phase once his work is completed.
