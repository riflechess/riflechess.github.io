#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Library Board Meeting
**Purpose:** Discussion of Staff Report 26-154 regarding Newcomer Services
**Date/Time:** October 7, 2026, at 1:00 PM
**Context:** Presentation by Newcomer Services Coordinator Adriana Galvan regarding programs for immigrants, refugees, and underserved communities in Saint Paul.

#### Summary
The meeting focused primarily on the "Newcomer Services" initiative, highlighting the library’s role as a trusted hub for information and essential services for the community.

**Key Services and Programs:**
*   **Welcoming Points:** The library provides information on legal providers, health services (preventative and dental), and digital access.
*   **Document Assistance:** The library offers free notarization for essential documents, such as Power of Attorney and DOPA forms, specifically for residents who may be facing financial hardships.
*   **Digital Literacy:** Offerings include education in Spanish, Korean, and specific programs tailored for the African American community.
*   **Cell Ed App:** The library currently utilizes the "Cell Ed" platform, which provides 24/7 access to live instructors (not AI) for those seeking to learn English, navigate citizenship processes, or take parenting classes.
*   **Community Outreach:** Staff members operate as "community specialists," meaning they often meet residents where they are—at bus stops, community centers, or churches—to provide reliable information and steer them away from common scams.

**Budget and Funding:**
*   **Current Staffing:** The Newcomer Services team is funded through the general fund.
*   **Mellon Foundation Grant:** A three-year grant was secured to fund additional staffing (a Spanish-speaking specialist and a general community specialist) to expand these services.
*   **Equipment/Apps:** The "Cell Ed" licenses and other specialized tools are currently supported through the Mellon grant, ensuring these programs continue for the near future.

**Public Safety and Quality of Life:**
*   **Trust & Scams:** A significant portion of the discussion centered on the library as a "safe space." Because vulnerable populations are often targets for scams, the library works to provide "trusted" information regarding legal and social services.
*   **Emergency Response:** The board acknowledged the library's successful role during "Operation Metro Search," where the library coordinated over 3,150 deliveries of basic needs (medicine, supplies, and food) for homebound families.

**Contention:**
There were no significant disagreements or divided votes during this meeting. The board expressed high levels of support and appreciation for the work performed by the community specialists.
