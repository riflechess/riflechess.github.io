#### Meeting Info
**Type:** City Council Meeting  
**Purpose:** Routine legislative business, budget presentations, and public hearings regarding utility franchises and infrastructure.  
**Date/Time:** September 16, 2026, at 3:30 PM  
**Location:** City of Saint Paul

#### Summary

**Safe Streets for All Grant**
The Council approved Resolution 26-1460, which allows the city to enter into a grant agreement with the Federal Highway Administration.
*   **Funding:** A total of $18.1 million ($14 million federal grant and $4 million state match).
*   **Purpose:** The funds are earmarked for approximately 40 miles of city and county roadways to improve high-visibility crosswalks, curb extensions, traffic circles, and other low-cost safety features.
*   **Timeline:** The project has a five-year implementation window starting in 2027.

**St. Paul Port Authority 2027 Budget**
The Port Authority presented its 2027 budget and shared updates on its operations as an economic engine for the city.
*   **Tax Levy:** The Port is requesting a $75,000 increase in the mandatory property tax levy to address the costs of inflation on personnel and benefits.
*   **TIF Status:** The Port reported success with several Tax Increment Financing (TIF) districts and plans to "decertify" several of them early, which will return roughly $66 million in property taxes to the general tax base.
*   **Infrastructure:** Significant projects are ongoing at the Barge Terminals (including a $18 million dock wall rehabilitation) and a study to resolve train blockages at the Southport Terminal.

**Utility Franchise Agreements (Xcel Energy / Northern States Power)**
Several ordinances were discussed regarding gas and electric franchise fees. 
*   **Contention/Detail:** During the public hearing for the gas franchise, a resident (Mr. Purdy) raised a significant concern regarding "equity" in the current agreement. While the agreement includes a 50% discount on franchise fees for low-income residents, the resident pointed out that "legacy buildings" (roughly 318 units) and certain high-rise buildings might be excluded from this discount because the utility's system categorizes them as commercial rather than single-family residential units. 
*   **Response:** Council members acknowledged this gap as an important concern regarding equity for residents in non-homestead, low-income rentals. Staff will be asked to provide a formal follow-up on how these "legacy" units will be treated in future assessments.

**Public Safety & Operations**
*   **Fire Department:** The Council approved resolutions to fund $30,000 for a state grant for an apprenticeship program and $5,467 for scrap metal recycling proceeds.
*   **Infrastructure/Noise:** A noise variance was approved for Xcel Energy to perform high-intensity work near the CPKC Railroad Crossing. This work involves drilling and jacking casings under the tracks and will require 24/7 operations for 2–3 weeks; however, the company committed to using sound mitigation barriers to protect nearby residents.
*   **Maintenance:** Standard maintenance and operations approvals were passed for the 7th Place Mall and the Grand Snelling parking lot.

**Community Updates**
*   **Facility Changes:** Upcoming community meetings are scheduled to discuss potential closures of the Duluth and Case Recreation Center and the Dayton's Bluff Library.
*   **Local Events:** Upcoming events include the Frogtown Arts Festival, the Capitol Fest, and a variety of local farmers' markets and voter education nights across the city.
