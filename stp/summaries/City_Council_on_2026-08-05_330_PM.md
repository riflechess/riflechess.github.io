#### Meeting Info
**Meeting Type:** City Council Meeting  
**Purpose:** Regular legislative session, budget adjustments, and public hearings  
**Date/Time:** August 5, 2026, at 3:30 PM  
**Location:** St. Paul City Hall

***

#### Summary

The council met to discuss several items ranging from tenant rights and labor relations to municipal budgets and park amenities. Several significant points of contention were noted regarding local safety and labor laws.

**Tenant Protections & Right to Repair (Failed Resolution)**
This was the most debated item of the evening. The council discussed a ballot initiative presented by "Safe Homes St. Paul" regarding tenant protections and a "right to repair." 
*   **The Conflict:** City attorneys warned that the ordinance as written may be unconstitutional or in conflict with state law, making it difficult to enforce if passed.
*   **The Debate:** Some council members argued that despite these legal concerns, the city should allow the residents of St. Paul to decide via a ballot initiative, emphasizing the importance of community engagement and tenant rights. Opposing members argued that placing an unenforceable or legally flawed ordinance on the ballot would mislead voters and "set them up for failure."
*   **Outcome:** The resolution failed with a vote of 2-5.

**Labor & Employment (Public Hearing)**
The council held a public hearing regarding the **Deputy City Clerk** position. There was significant pushback from local labor unions (AFSCME Local 2508).
*   **Community Concern:** Union representatives and residents testified that moving this role to an "unclassified" status would be seen as "union-busting." They argued that there is no legal necessity for the position to be non-represented and that current union staff already perform high-level duties successfully.
*   **Status:** This item was moved to a "lay over" until August 12th for final consideration.

**Public Safety & Infrastructure Improvements**
Several items related to public safety and infrastructure were approved unanimously:
*   **Hazardous Materials:** A $360,000 agreement was approved with the State of Minnesota to provide hazardous materials response capabilities.
*   **Emergency Services:** Funding for training/equipment for the Minnesota Air Rescue Team ($500,000) and a reimbursement from the City of Edina were both approved.
*   **Road Safety:** A $21,131 payment was accepted to settle terms with the Minnesota Task Force 1.
*   **EV Infrastructure:** The city accepted a $50,000 grant for the installation of two curbside EV charging stations.

**Parks & Recreation / Quality of Life**
*   **Golf Course Expansion:** New rules were adopted to allow "mini-golf" and permit golf courses (specifically the Highland National Driving Range) to operate until 10:00 PM with lights, aimed at increasing community access.
*   **Trail Connections:** $250,000 was allocated for improving trail connections near the Mounds maintenance facility.
*   **Zoo/Conservatory Support:** A large donation of $2.28 million was approved to support the Como Zoo and Marjorie McNeely Conservatory.
*   **Sound Variances:** The council granted sound variances for "India Fest 2026" and for amplified music at Wabasha Brewing Company.

**Economic Development & Property**
*   **Waste Management:** A new ordinance requires City Council approval for any mixed municipal solid waste contracts.
*   **Vacant Buildings:** Regarding the property at 1047 Arcade Street, the council granted a 90-day waiver on vacant building fees to allow time for the owner to clear code issues before potentially opening as a cannabis facility.
*   **Minimum Wage:** A hearing was held regarding the removal of a 90-day minimum wage exception for minors (ages 14-17), which will be reviewed again on August 12th.
