#### Meeting Info
*   **Meeting Type:** City Council and Library Board Meetings
*   **Purpose:** Review of city ordinances, budget levies, franchise agreements, and public safety issues.
*   **Date/Time:** September 23, 2026, at 3:30 PM
*   **Location:** City of Saint Paul

#### Summary
**Audit & Public Works**
The Council approved the selection of two audit topics for 2027: business licensing operations and public works (specifically street maintenance and pothole repair). These areas were chosen based on frequent community concerns.

**Business Licensing & Public Safety**
*   **Tobacco Shop Penalty:** Resolution 26-1553 was passed to impose a $1,000 fine on Speedway No. 4420 (located at 11257th Street West). The business was cited for a second violation involving the sale of tobacco to a minor. While the owner presented a defense regarding training and management changes, the Council upheld the fine to ensure enforcement of anti-tobacco policies.
*   **Nuisance Abatement:** Regarding the property at 2203 Selby Avenue, there was a factual dispute regarding whether the site had been sufficiently improved. The owner requested a two-week extension to remove a travel trailer and clear other debris. The Council granted the two-week extension before moving toward forced abatement.

**Community & Proclamations**
*   **Dolly Parton Day:** The Council unanimously proclaimed September 25, 2026, as "Dolly Parton Day" in the city of St. Paul to celebrate her impact on music and her advocacy for working people.

**Budget & Tax Levies**
*   **Library Levy:** The Library Board met to set the maximum levy for 2027. There was significant discussion regarding the inclusion of the Dayton's Bluff Library and other rec centers in the budget. Council Member Johnson voiced strong opposition to the initial proposal of cutting these services, arguing that such amenities are essential for the community. A supplemental budget from the Mayor has been used to protect these services.
*   **Property Tax Levy:** The Council approved a maximum property tax levy of 6.8% for the city. While this is the "ceiling" for the budget, members emphasized that they are committed to finding ways to lower the impact on residents during the final budget drafting process.

**Infrastructure & Business Agreements**
*   **Energy Franchise Fees:** The Council approved several ordinances (26-40, 26-41, 26-43, and 26-44) regarding XL Energy. These agreements include a "progressive franchise fee" that provides a 50% discount on energy bills for residents enrolled in assistance programs. The terms also allow for more frequent negotiations with the provider.
*   **Grant Funding:** The city approved several grants for projects including the "Ember on Pine" development, the Como Park Zoo and Conservatory, and various regional water service improvements.
*   **Property Conveyance:** The council moved to streamline the process of transferring city-owned land to the Board of Water Commissioners for infrastructure use.

**Ward Updates**
*   **Retail:** The downtown Aldi location is scheduled to open on September 24.
*   **Events:** Upcoming local events include Selby Days (Sept 26) and the Marydale Festival (Sept 26).
