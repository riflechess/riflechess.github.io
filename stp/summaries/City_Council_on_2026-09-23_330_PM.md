#### Meeting Info
**Type:** City Council & Library Board Meeting
**Purpose:** Discussion of audit selections, licensing penalties, 2027 budget levies, franchise fee negotiations, and various public works projects.
**Date/Time:** September 23, 2026, at 3:30 PM

#### Summary

**Tobacco Shop Penalty**
The Council reviewed a case involving a local tobacco shop (Speedway No. 4420) that was fined $1,000 for selling products to a minor. This was the business’s second violation in a short timeframe. While the business owner testified regarding their commitment to staff training and new internal systems to prevent future occurrences, the Council upheld the $1,000 penalty to ensure compliance with anti-tobacco and youth-protection policies.

**Budget and Property Tax Levies**
Significant time was spent discussing the 2027 property tax levies. 
*   **The 6.8% Maximum Levy:** The Council approved a maximum property tax levy of 6.8%. While this is the "ceiling" for the budget, officials emphasized that they will continue to work on ways to reduce this number in the coming months.
*   **Library and Rec Center Protection:** There was significant internal discussion regarding the budget. Councilmembers expressed strong opposition to any cuts that would impact essential public services, specifically naming the Dayton's Bluff Library and various recreation centers (like Highwood Hills). A "supplemental budget" was utilized to ensure these sites remain fully funded and operational.
*   **Advocacy for Residents:** Council members highlighted the challenge of rising costs and the importance of keeping local "basics"—such as libraries, roads, and public safety—as a priority in the upcoming budget cycle.

**Franchise Fees (XL Energy)**
The Council approved several updates regarding the energy franchise fee. A key outcome of these negotiations is a "progressive franchise fee," which will provide a 50% discount on electricity bills for residents enrolled in energy assistance programs. This is intended to assist low-income households and ensure utility costs remain manageable.

**Public Works & Safety**
*   **Roads and Safety:** The council discussed and approved funding for street lighting maintenance and a project for the replacement of regional water services.
*   **Salt Storage:** A public hearing was held regarding updates to salt storage regulations.
*   **Nuisance Abatement:** A specific property at 2203 Selby Avenue was addressed regarding a "nuisance" (a trailer and boat in a backyard). The owner, citing health issues, requested a two-week extension to clear the property. The Council granted this extension to allow the owner more time to coordinate with neighbors to move the items.

**Community Proclamations and Grants**
*   **Dolly Parton Day:** The city officially proclaimed September 25, 2026, as "Dolly Parton Day" to celebrate the singer's impact on culture and her advocacy for working people.
*   **Grant Funding:** Several grants were approved for community improvements, including $2.9 million for the Como Park Zoo and Conservatory, and a grant for the "Ember on Pine" project at 500 Broadway Street.

**Audit Selection**
The council approved the selection of two key areas for the 2027 audit cycle: business licensing operations and public works' street maintenance and pothole repair operations.
