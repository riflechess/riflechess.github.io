#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Webinar / Information Session  
**Purpose:** To provide instructions and details for organizations wishing to apply for 2027 professional service contracts with the Office of Neighborhood Safety (ONS).  
**Context:** The session outlined five specific areas of need for upcoming contract awards, including violence prevention, homelessness support, and mental health outreach.

#### Summary
The webinar focused on the application process for 2027 professional service agreements funded by the Office of Neighborhood Safety (ONS). These contracts are intended to support community-based organizations in providing vital services to St. Paul residents.

**Key Funding Areas & Services:**
The ONS identified five specific areas where they will award contracts:
*   **Culturally Specific Violence Prevention:** Programs focusing on youth and multi-cultural community engagement (specifically targeting the Mong, Karen, Somali, Latino, and African-American communities).
*   **Stabilization for Unhoused Neighbors:** Services for individuals frequently using shelters or facing housing instability.
*   **Chemical and Mental Health Outreach:** Expanding services for those 25 and older to interrupt drug use/distribution in public spaces like parks and libraries.
*   **Capacity Building:** Targeted at small organizations (less than 10 years of history) to improve their internal processes and program reach.
*   **Justice Involvement Support:** Workforce development and education for individuals at risk of entering the justice system.

**Contract and Budget Details:**
*   **Budget Limits:** Each contract is capped at **$40,000**, with a minimum requirement of **$10,000**. 
*   **Nature of Funding:** These are professional service agreements (not grants), meaning the city is hiring organizations to perform specific work. 
*   **Payment Model:** Contracts operate on a reimbursement basis; providers perform services and invoice the office for costs incurred.
*   **Contractor Support:** Selected partners will participate in "cohort learning," providing them with capacity-building coaching and a supportive peer network of other contractors.

**Public Safety & Quality of Life:**
A significant portion of the discussion centered on improving local quality of life through:
*   Reducing gun violence and community violence via Project Peace.
*   Supporting the "Familiar Faces" initiative to get unhoused neighbors off the streets safely.
*   Implementing strategies to ensure public spaces (libraries, parks) remain safe for all residents.

**Q&A Highlights & Clarifications:**
During the Q&A session, a concern was raised regarding how current city budget deficits might impact these contracts. The representative confirmed that **funding for 2027 is secured**, and the office has made necessary adjustments to ensure community-based organizations are not impacted by the broader city's financial situation.

**Important Dates:**
*   **August 28, 2024:** Deadline for submitting questions via email.
*   **September 30, 2024 (approximate):** Release of FAQs and evaluation criteria.
*   **September 25, 2026:** Organizations will be notified of acceptance or denial.
