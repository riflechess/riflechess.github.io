#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Public Working Group Meeting
**Purpose:** Discussion of the Administrative Citations Implementation process.
**Location:** City Hall, Basement Room 41
**Key Speaker:** Tim Greenfield, Chief Policy Officer to the City Council.

#### Summary
The meeting focused on the development of an administrative citation ordinance. This is intended to create a "middle ground" enforcement tool for the city. Currently, St. Paul is an outlier in Minnesota as it lacks the authority to issue civil fines for non-criminal violations. The proposed system would allow the city to issue fines for various violations where a criminal charge is not appropriate, but a simple warning is insufficient to ensure compliance.

**Key Points and Policy Goals:**
*   **Targeted Issues:** Potential areas for enforcement include building codes, animal control, tenant protections, and "quality of life" issues.
*   **Enforcement Ladder:** The goal is to move away from binary choices (warning vs. criminal citation) and toward a tiered system that allows for an "opportunity to correct" before a fine is issued.
*   **Equity and Fairness:** Per the city charter, all fines must be "equitable." The working group is specifically looking at how to account for "ability to pay" and ensuring the process is fair for those with different economic circumstances.
*   **Legislative Process:** The ordinance will go through a three-reading process. The city intends to bundle the general ordinance with a specific list of 5–7 initial enforcement areas (such as property maintenance or code violations) to be presented for a public hearing.

**Current Status:**
The ordinance is currently being drafted by the city attorney’s office. The working group’s role is to provide public perspective and feedback on the system's design, specifically regarding the hearing process, the role of hearing officers, and how to ensure the system remains impartial and accessible to all residents.
