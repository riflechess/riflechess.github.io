#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Public Working Group Meeting
**Purpose:** To discuss and provide community feedback on the implementation of administrative citations within the City of Saint Paul.
**Location:** City Hall, Basement Room 41
**Context:** The meeting was led by Tim Greenfield, the Chief Policy Officer to the City Council, to walk the working group through the legislative process, the goals of the program, and the specific areas where the city intends to use administrative citation authority.

#### Summary
The primary focus of this meeting was to introduce the "Administrative Citations" program, which the city plans to use as a "middle ground" enforcement tool. This system is intended to bridge the gap between simple warnings/educational opportunities and high-level criminal citations.

**Key Points of Discussion:**
*   **Legislative Process:** The ordinance is currently being drafted by the City Attorney’s office. The city intends to use a three-reading process to move the ordinance forward. A target date of October 7th was mentioned for introduction, with a goal of full passage by June.
*   **Purpose of Citations:** The goal is to ensure compliance with city codes in a timely and consistent manner. It is not meant to replace existing enforcement tools but to provide a more proportionate response for violations that do not currently warrant criminal prosecution.
*   **Targeted Areas for Enforcement:** The city is currently identifying specific areas where these citations will be applied. Potential areas include:
    *   **Labor & Safety:** Minimum wage, wage theft, and safety violations.
    *   **Housing & Property:** Building codes, tenant protections, rent stabilization, and short-term rental (Airbnb) regulations.
    *   **Public Safety & Quality of Life:** Animal control, sidewalk and driveway permits, and skyway ordinance violations.
*   **Equity and Accessibility:** A significant portion of the discussion focused on ensuring that the program is applied equitably. This includes:
    *   **Ability to Pay:** Establishing a way to consider the financial circumstances of individuals when citations are issued.
    *   **The Appeals Process:** Ensuring the hearing process is impartial and that residents have a clear, accessible pathway to contest a citation.
    *   **Fairness:** Ensuring the system is "entry-level" and accessible to all residents, regardless of their resources.

**Points of Contention/Detailed Discussion:**
While no formal vote was taken during this meeting, there was significant discussion regarding the **equity of the appeals process**. The group discussed how to ensure that hearing officers remain neutral and how the city can manage "ability to pay" without creating a system that treats different groups differently. There is a specific focus on ensuring that residents with financial hardships have a mechanism to argue their circumstances during an appeal.
