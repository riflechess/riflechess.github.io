#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Housing & Redevelopment Authority (HRA) Meeting
**Purpose:** Review of parking agreements, loan modifications, TIF district amendments, and the 2027 budget presentation.
**Date/Time:** November 18, 2026, at 2:00 PM
**Location:** St. Paul (Commissioners participated in-person and remotely)

#### Summary
The meeting covered several key updates regarding local infrastructure, commercial support, and long-term tax planning. All votes taken during the meeting were unanimous.

**Key Decisions & Budget Items:**
*   **Parking & Business Support:** 
    *   The HRA approved a parking use agreement for the Lawson parking ramp (10 West 6th Street).
    *   A loan modification was approved for the Model Cities Brownstone commercial project (837 University Avenue West) in partnership with the St. Paul and Minnesota Foundation.
*   **Tax Increment Financing (TIF) Reductions:** 
    *   The board approved a series of amendments to several TIF districts (Phelan Village and Riverfront Renaissance) and the full decertification of the Emerald Park and Pioneer Endicott districts.
    *   **Impact:** These actions will return approximately **$211 million** in market value to the general tax base. This represents a roughly 10% reduction in the amount of tax capacity captured by these TIF districts, as the associated developments are considered complete.
*   **Property Sale:** A public hearing was held and approved for the sale of an HRA-owned parcel at 515 LaFond Avenue.
*   **2027 Budget Update:** 
    *   Staff presented a series of "technical changes" for the 2027 budget. 
    *   Key updates included the cleanup of "unspent" funds in the Housing Trust Fund and the Loan Enterprise Fund, and the adjustment of costs for EV chargers and the "Full-stack" program.
    *   A specific discussion was held regarding the "Listening House" funding, ensuring it is correctly categorized in the general fund.

**Public Safety & Quality of Life:**
*   **Affordable Housing Focus:** There was extensive discussion regarding the balance between using TIF as a "creative financing tool" for growth and the need to ensure St. Paul remains affordable for current residents. Commissioners emphasized that while TIF is vital for new development, it must be balanced with efforts to keep property taxes manageable for those relying on local services.
*   **Housing Production:** A point was raised regarding the current split between "production" (new building), "preservation" (keeping existing housing), and "protection" (advocacy/support) for affordable housing.
