#### Meeting Info
**Type:** Budget Committee Meeting
**Purpose:** Review of EV car share/charging infrastructure costs and updates on District Council funding structures.
**Date/Time:** August 5, 2026, at 10:00 AM

#### Summary

**EV Car Share & Charging Infrastructure**
The committee discussed the budget and operational logistics for the city's electric vehicle (EV) infrastructure. Key points included:
*   **Budget Request:** A proposal was presented for approximately **$440,000 above base** to cover operating costs for the charging network, EV car share program, and expansion funds related to the CMAC 3 program. This is part of a larger $1.1 million regional effort shared with neighboring counties and Minneapolis.
*   **Operational Costs & Concerns:** There was significant discussion regarding high insurance premiums (which accounted for about 23% of annual expenses) and the "bananas" cost of commercial rates from the service provider, Excel. To combat this, the city is exploring ways to move insurance under a municipal fleet policy and has implemented an analytics camera system to improve driver accountability and lower risk.
*   **Maintenance:** Costs for cable repairs spiked in 2024 due to frequent cuts, but new service agreements (specifically with Zef Energy) are expected to stabilize these costs moving forward.

**District Councils Funding & Strategy**
The committee received a detailed update on how the city supports its 17 District Councils:
*   **Funding Shift:** A major shift occurred between the 2025 and 2026 budget cycles where **CDBG (federal) funding was removed** from the district council support. This move was intended to reduce bureaucracy and allow councils more flexibility in how they spend money on community engagement without restrictive federal limits.
*   **Budget Increase:** The total allocation for District Councils is moving toward roughly **$1.7 million** for 2026 (up from ~$1.4 million). A one-time transfer of $345,000 from a parking fund was used to facilitate the move away from CDBG funding.
*   **Equity & Outreach:** There is ongoing discussion regarding how these funds are used to overcome barriers for marginalized residents, such as providing language access and other forms of community outreach.

A community engagement event regarding the budget is scheduled for **August 13th from 5:30 PM to 7:00 PM at the Battle Creek Rec Center**.
