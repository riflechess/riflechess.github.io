#### Meeting Info
**Type:** City Council Meeting
**Purpose:** Routine Business, Appointments, and Public Hearings
**Date/Time:** November 18, 2026, at 3:30 PM
**Context:** The meeting included the appointment of a new Fire Chief, several grant approvals for public safety, and various property-related zoning variances.

#### Summary
**Key Appointments and Community Items**
*   **New Fire Chief:** The Council unanimously approved the appointment of Jeremiah Melquist as the new Chief of the St. Paul Fire Department. The appointment followed significant testimony from department leadership and community advocates who praised Melquist's experience and commitment to the city.
*   **Mobile Saunas:** An ordinance was passed to allow mobile saunas in city parks and to establish a licensing fee structure for them.
*   **Community Appointments:** Several individuals were appointed to various boards, including the Parks and Recreation Commission and the Mayor's Advisory Council for People with Disabilities.

**Public Safety and Technology (Contention Areas)**
*   **CCTV Improvement Grant (Item 42):** There was significant discussion regarding a grant from the Department of Justice to improve the city's CCTV system. 
    *   **The Dispute:** While the department argued the grant is intended to maintain existing equipment rather than expand surveillance, Council Member Kim voted against the proposal. She expressed concerns regarding the lack of transparency in how the funds would be spent and the potential for "overreach" regarding AI-enabled technology.
    *   **Result:** The vote was not unanimous (5-1).
*   **Records Management System (Item 43):** Similar concerns were raised regarding a grant to update the city's records management system. The Council decided to **lay this item over** until the next meeting to allow for a clearer breakdown of the contract with the vendor (Axon) and to ensure the city is not becoming overly dependent on a single provider for data and security.
*   **Police Commissioner Stipends (Item 38):** A resolution was discussed to increase stipends for the Police Civilian Internal Affairs Review Commission.
    *   **The Dispute:** Council Member Johnson expressed concern over the specific wording of the ordinance, noting that it may lack transparency by allowing a director to set stipends at their discretion. While she supports the pay increase, she did not support the current drafting of the motion.
    *   **Result:** The vote was not unanimous (5-1).

**Zoning and Property Matters**
*   **Fence Variances (Items 50 & 51):** These items involved specific local disputes regarding fence heights and safety. 
    *   One case involved a "nuisance" fence that required repairs to be deemed safe by the city.
    *   Another involved a request for a height variance to address concerns regarding privacy and "voyeurism."
*   **Rezoning (Item 34):** The Council voted to **deny** the rezoning of 1201 Rice Street from T2 to B3, citing the need to maintain the area as a high-density, transit-oriented neighborhood rather than allowing a transition to auto-related businesses.

**Other Notes**
*   **Garbage Services (Item 35):** The city will begin implementing updated rates and procedures for base-level garbage service starting January 1, 2027.
*   **Sustainable Building (Item 37):** The council reviewed a first reading to update the Sustainable Building Ordinance, focusing on raising the threshold for requirements to larger projects.
